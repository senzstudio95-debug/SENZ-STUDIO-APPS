SENZ Buddy now uses the supplied SENZ chibi character as a real textured 3D model, including the minimized desktop Buddy and matching toolbar icon.

- Procedural breathing, waving, walking, turning, jumping, dancing and reaction motions. The source OBJ has no skeleton or facial blend shapes; these are lightweight mesh animations, not prerecorded skeletal clips.
- Geometry reduced from 1,363,862 to 24,000 triangles, with a bundled 2048px texture. Works offline.
- Lazy loading, 30 FPS cap, bounded resolution, animation paused when hidden, reduced-motion support, and a still-image fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
- Preserves Order Pro, existing projects, fonts and settings. Installer must remain below 400,000,000 bytes.
- Removes a leftover reference to the retired DS app in the license-expiry cleanup.
