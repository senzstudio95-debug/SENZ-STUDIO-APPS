How to use

  1. Open TikTok Shop orders and expand customer messages.
  2. Click the SENZ icon and enter a product name shown in the orders.
  3. Click Scan Order. Matching orders are added once per order ID.
  4. Review the name, font, size, color and quantity. Mark each order Verified after checking.
  5. Export selected orders to CSV or JSON.

No TikTok API login is required. The extension reads page text, not OCR. Hidden messages, multiple products and different page layouts may require manual review.
